The bedrock

“Read.”

The Holy Quran, in its very first word, asked the Prophet Mohammed SAW, and through him all humankind, to read and to learn. He made the pursuit of knowledge an obligation upon every Muslim man and woman.

Imam Mohammed al-Baqir AS and Imam Ja‘far al-Sadiq AS set out the jurisprudence they inherited and trained a generation of jurists. The epistles of Ikhwān al-Safā’, by Imam Ahmad al-Taqi AS, showed every discipline, seen rightly, to be a path to the oneness of Allah.

The realisation

Darse Saifee becomes Aljamea-tus-Saifiyah

A century after its founding, the academy passed to Syedna Taher Saifuddin RA, in an age of world wars, independence movements and unheard-of scientific discovery. He reshaped its teaching so graduates could thrive in that world.

In 1381/1961 he built the new Surat campus, welcomed English, the sciences and the humanities, and renamed the academy Jāmi‘a, Arabic for university, and Saifiyya, honouring both Syednas who shaped it.
